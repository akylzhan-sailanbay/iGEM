The files here are the Latin and Latin Extended subsets, taken from the Fontsource packages
`@fontsource-variable/mona-sans` and `@fontsource-variable/source-serif-4`. They are self-hosted
because iGEM does not allow wikis to load resources from external services such as font CDNs.
